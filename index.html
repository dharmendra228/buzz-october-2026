Chapter 2 of 10: AI Research

AI Adoption to AI Contribution

Our Research Paper Published in Bloomsbury

“From AI adoption to AI contribution.”

An AI-first culture is one where we do not just use AI, but actively explore, question, experiment, collaborate, and contribute to the evolving conversation around it.

In that spirit, we are proud to share that an original research paper authored by Dr. Rima Ghose Chowdhury, Sanjeet Banerji, and Dr. Ashutosh Kumar Pandey has been published by Bloomsbury in Digital Strategies and Transformation: Strategic Innovations for a Tech-Driven Economy (SEEDS 2025).

Titled “A Study on Assessing the Relationship between Industry Sector and Belief in AI Adoption for Leadership Decision-Making,” the paper examines how industry context shapes leaders’ views on AI adoption. It also presents a practical AI Adoption Framework spanning Strategy, Data, Technology, People, and Governance & Ethics, supported by key enablers including Culture, Leadership, and Ecosystem Partnerships.

The AI Adoption Framework

  • Strategy
  • Data
  • Technology
  • People
  • Governance & Ethics

Dr. Rima Ghose Chowdhury shares insights at the Inaugural CII Western Region CHRO Summit 2026

“From Pilots to Practice: Making Agentic AI Work Inside HR”

Dr. Rima Ghose Chowdhury, President – CHRO & CSR Leader, Datamatics, was invited as Panelist at the Inaugural CII Western Region CHRO Summit 2026, themed “The Modern CHRO Playbook.”

The session on Agentic AI & Technology – “From Pilots to Practice: Making Agentic AI Work Inside HR” explored the practical applications, the importance of building reskilling capabilities at the pace of change, and the evolving role of HR in navigating technology-led transformation.

Lean Six Sigma White Belt Training

The first batch of the Lean Six Sigma White Belt Training Programme was conducted from 12th to 16th October 2026, with 16 participants from Mumbai, Nashik, Bangalore, and Puducherry successfully completing the training.

Spanning 15 hours over five consecutive days, the programme equipped participants with foundational knowledge of Lean Six Sigma principles and their practical application in the workplace. Having completed the training, participants are now in the process of identifying suitable projects and are expected to implement and complete them over the next 2–3 months, translating their learning into tangible business improvements.

Batch 2 Update: The second batch is currently underway from 26th to 30th October 2026, continuing the organisation-wide initiative to build process improvement capabilities across locations.

Tech # Talk — API Management Gateway at Mumbai

As part of its continued efforts to promote technology awareness and encourage knowledge sharing, the Business and Corporate Learning & Development Team organized a Tech# Talk session on API Gateway (APG) in October, led by Siddhesh Malvankar.

The session provided participants with an understanding of API Gateway fundamentals, its role in managing API communication, security and access control policies, authentication, traffic management, and integration with multiple backend services. Through practical examples and interactive discussions, the session highlighted how API Gateways enable secure, efficient, and seamless communication between applications.

The Tech# Talks initiative continues to foster a culture of continuous learning, innovation, and knowledge sharing, empowering employees to explore emerging technologies and strengthen their technical capabilities.

Celebrating Service, Strengthening Connections: Customer Service Week 2026

We celebrated Customer Service Week with a series of engaging activities honouring our employees’ dedication, resilience, and valuable contributions. The week featured a meaningful Mental Health Session that highlighted the importance of well-being, followed by an exciting Trivia Night and a thrilling Solving Mystery Night that encouraged teamwork, critical thinking, and friendly competition.

In Manila, the celebration culminated in a Recognition Night, giving the team an opportunity to acknowledge outstanding contributions and celebrate the people behind every successful customer experience. Meanwhile, the Cebu team shared the joy with snacks, adding a simple yet thoughtful touch to the celebration.

More than a week of fun and appreciation, Customer Service Week reminded everyone that exceptional service starts with empowered, supported, and valued employees. Through these activities, Datamatics strengthened team connections, celebrated individual and collective achievements, and reinforced its commitment to fostering a positive and appreciative workplace culture.

Smash, Rally, Connect: Cebu Badminton Tournament Day

The Cebu Badminton Tournament Day brought Datamaticians together at Point21 Badminton Court in Cebu for a full day of exciting matches, friendly competition, and sportsmanship. Participants stepped away from work to showcase their skills, challenge themselves, and cheer on colleagues. Energetic rallies and hard-fought points fostered enthusiasm and camaraderie. Beyond the games, the event strengthened relationships, encouraged teamwork, and promoted an active, balanced lifestyle, leaving participants with lasting memories and a renewed sense of unity beyond the workplace.

By fostering connection and an active, balanced lifestyle, the event aligned with the Datamatics Wheel of wellbeing.

Beyond the Waves: Building Bonds, Creating Memories with IT Department Team Building Activity

After months of planning with Dive Spot PH and House of Goodies, the Datamatics IT Team Building brought the team to Puerto De Ligpo, San Luis, Batangas, for two days of adventure, relaxation, and camaraderie. Marine exploration, beachside relaxation, Trivia Night, and drawing and guessing games sparked laughter and friendly competition. Supported by seamless coordination, dedicated partners, and enthusiastic participation, the event strengthened connections between leadership and technical teams and created lasting memories.
